## Audience

Corporate decision-makers need confidence that `{{ customer.name }}` can use Locally Twisted for a polished public-facing event.

## Answer First

Put event goal, proposed headline moments, investment range, proof angle, and next approval step near the front so the buyer can explain the value internally.

## Required Data

- Event goal and audience
- Proposed decor moments: entrance, stage, tables, photo moment, sponsor or brand areas
- Relevant proof photos from schools, healthcare, universities, dealerships, and community events
- Scope, timing, assumptions, and investment range or quote
- Approval steps, vendor setup path, and next meeting/contact

## Recipient Outcome

The buyer can recommend Locally Twisted internally, justify the expense, and move toward approval or a recurring contract.

## Automation Notes

This is where civic, patriotic, growth-positioning, and portfolio proof can be richer than the invoice.

Do not auto-send from this registry. A human must approve proof photos, client logo/name usage, proposal copy, pricing, and any contract language.

## Boundaries

Do not imply client endorsement from past corporate names unless the source and permission are approved. Do not use unreviewed photos or brand marks.
