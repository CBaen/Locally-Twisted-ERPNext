THE PROCESS

Booking is straightforward.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your event

    Date, location, guest count, and what you’re imagining. Use the form below or call. We respond same day on weekdays.

  2. 02

    We confirm availability and scope

    We’ll let you know if the date works and recommend the right team size for your crowd. A quote follows.

  3. 03

    Deposit holds your date

    A deposit reserves the team. The balance is due closer to the event. We send a confirmation with all logistics.

  4. 04

    We show up and work the room

    We arrive early, set up, and run for the agreed duration. You don’t manage us — that’s not your job at the party.

Common questions

How is pricing calculated?

Each artist is $130 for the first hour and $115 for each additional hour. Twisting and face painting are priced the same way. Tax is added at checkout.

Why don’t you offer a multi-artist discount?

Each artist is creating custom art for your event — two artists means twice the creative work. We price each one independently so we can keep the quality of every minute they’re with you.

Can I book on short notice?

Yes — last-minute is one of our specialties. We’ve taken bookings even 24 hours out. Give us a call at (801) 285-0860 or send us a message and we’ll find a way to make it work.

What deposit do you require?

$50 per artist when you book — that secures your date. Corporate clients don’t pay a deposit; we invoice on Net 30 after the event.

When is the balance due?

The remaining balance is due 72 hours before your event. We’ll send a reminder.

How does sales tax work?

Utah sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery or event city. The combined rate is typically around 7.25%. Quoted prices don’t include tax — it’s added as its own line item so you can see exactly what you’re paying.
