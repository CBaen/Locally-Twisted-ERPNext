Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often. If yours isn’t here, give us a call — we’d rather talk it through than have you guess.

Booking and pricing

How is pricing calculated?

Each artist is $130 for the first hour and $115 for each additional hour. Twisting and face painting are priced the same way. Tax is added at checkout.

Why don’t you offer a multi-artist discount?

Each artist is creating custom art for your event — two artists means twice the creative work. We price each one independently so we can keep the quality of every minute they’re with you.

What deposit do you require?

  • Face painting and balloon twisting: $50 per artist when you book.
  • Balloon decor (arches, columns, large installations): paid in full at booking.
  • Small deliveries under $100 (bouquets, gift cups, themed kits): paid in full at order.
  • Corporate clients: no deposit — we invoice on Net 30 after the event.

When is the balance due?

For face painting and balloon twisting events, the remaining balance is due 72 hours before your event. We’ll send a reminder.

What if I’m a corporate client?

Corporate clients don’t pay a deposit. We invoice you after the event on Net 30 terms. If an invoice goes unpaid past 30 days, we may add a 10% simple late fee on the original balance — though we often waive it for ongoing partnerships.

How does sales tax work?

Utah sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery or event city. The combined rate is typically around 7.25% (state plus local). Quoted prices don’t include tax — it’s added as its own line item so you can see exactly what you’re paying.

Cancellation and rescheduling

What’s your cancellation policy?

See our refund and cancellation policy for the full details. The short version: deposits for face painting and twisting transfer to a new date if you cancel 72+ hours out; balloon decor is fully refundable up to 7 days before the event.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. If your deposit transfers, you have 6 months from your original event date to use it on a new booking.

What happens if outdoor weather affects our event?

You decide whether to cancel for weather. If weather affects the event once we’re already setting up or running, we don’t issue a partial refund — we’ll work with you on a plan, but the event is happening.

Service area

What areas do you serve?

We deliver without a travel fee throughout Davis, Weber, Salt Lake, and Utah counties along the Wasatch Front.

Do you charge for travel?

If your event is outside those four counties, a travel fee applies. The amount depends on distance — call us at (801) 285-0860 or email hi@locallytwisted.com for a quote.

Themes and characters

Can your artists do a specific character or theme?

Yes — just ask. SpongeBob, Patrick, Squidward, unicorn, Spider-Man, anything the kid dreams up. We send experienced twisters and face painters to private parties; they have the skill range to make whatever the request is. There are no character restrictions.
