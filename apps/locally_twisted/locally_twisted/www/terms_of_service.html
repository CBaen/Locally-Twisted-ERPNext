Terms of Service

Working with Locally Twisted

These terms explain how bookings, orders, payments, cancellations, and service areas work for Locally Twisted.

Quotes, orders, and booking

  • Submitting a form does not lock in an event date. Your booking is confirmed when Locally Twisted accepts the job and the required payment or deposit is complete.
  • Custom decor, face painting, and balloon twisting are scheduled based on availability, event details, location, and scope.
  • For jobs that require a contract, payment of the invoice is treated as acceptance of the booking terms unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Payment terms

  • Face painting and balloon twisting: $50 per artist deposit when you book. The remaining balance is due 72 hours before your event.
  • Personal balloon decor: 100% payment upfront before preparation begins.
  • Small deliveries under $100: 100% payment at order.
  • Corporate clients: no deposit; Net 30 invoicing after the event unless we agree otherwise.
  • Late corporate invoices: we may add a 10% simple late fee on the original balance after Net 30. We may waive this case by case.

Cancellations, refunds, and rescheduling

Cancellation and refund rules are posted on our Refund Policy. If a deposit transfers to a new event date, the new date must be within 6 months of the original event date.

Service area and travel fees

Locally Twisted serves Davis, Weber, Salt Lake, and Utah counties without an additional travel fee. Events outside those counties may include a travel fee, quoted case by case.

Outdoor events and weather

The client decides whether to cancel for weather. If weather affects an event once we are already setting up or running the service, we do not issue partial refunds for the time or setup already committed.

Client responsibilities

Please provide accurate event details, a reachable on-site contact, reasonable venue access, and any access notes we need for parking, loading, setup, or service timing. Details vary by event and may be confirmed during quoting.

Website use

Please use this site for normal customer inquiries, bookings, orders, and account activity. Do not interfere with the site, attempt unauthorized access, submit false information, or use the site in a way that disrupts service for others.

Questions

Call (801) 285-0860 or email hi@locallytwisted.com before booking if anything here is unclear.
