About Locally Twisted

Balloon decor built for Utah events since 1998.

Custom balloon decor, delivery and install support, balloon twisting, and face painting for Wasatch Front events.

A Utah balloon company for real event days.

Locally Twisted helps Utah events look finished, festive, and intentional without putting every setup detail on the host. The team creates custom balloon decor for corporate events, schools, civic gatherings, private celebrations, venues, and community moments across the Wasatch Front.

The work ranges from arches, columns, garlands, backdrops, bouquets, drops, and centerpieces to live balloon twisting and face painting. Some projects are simple and quick; others need delivery timing, installation planning, venue access, color matching, and a quote before the event can move forward safely.
