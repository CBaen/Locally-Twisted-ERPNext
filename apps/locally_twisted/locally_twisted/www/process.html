Step by step

How an event balloon project moves from idea to install.

The details change by venue and event, but the operating shape stays steady: we get the right facts, design to the room, confirm the plan, and show up prepared.

Quote request

Send the date, venue or city, audience, rough budget if you have one, and any color or theme direction.

Planning call or message thread

We clarify goals, timing, access, ceiling or wall limits, outdoor exposure, delivery windows, and teardown needs.

Design and quote

We recommend the right form: arch, garland, columns, backdrop, stage frame, bouquet set, or custom build.

Confirm and schedule

Once scope is approved, we confirm production details, event timing, delivery address, and payment path.

Delivery and install

Our team arrives prepared, installs cleanly, checks placement, and leaves the event space ready for guests.

Teardown, invoice, and follow-up

When teardown or invoicing is part of the project, we handle it as part of the event workflow.

Payment and business support

Ready-to-order checkout and event quotes are intentionally different.
