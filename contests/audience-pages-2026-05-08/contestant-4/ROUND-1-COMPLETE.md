# Round 1 Complete — Contestant 4

## Concept summary

One system, four buyers. The four pages are structurally consistent — hero, proof layer, case/category proof, gallery, client roster, service note, CTA — but diverge in the ways that matter for each buyer. The civic page feels civic. The corporate page feels professional and AP-ready. The schools page is spirit-driven and schedule-respecting. The private page is personal and milestone-warm without crossing into cute or sticker-y.

The key compositional choice: the private celebrations page replaces the named-client roster with a testimonials section using real Google reviews from `home.py` — because that buyer makes decisions on peer trust, not logo lists. And the memorial testimonial ("sports themed funeral stand...very tasteful and meaningful") is placed deliberately because it's the most emotionally resonant proof in the entire review set, and this is the page where it belongs.

Every page uses the approved palette, approved icons, approved typography, hero contract enforced, no `!important`, no global CSS additions, no invented clients, no lorem ipsum.
