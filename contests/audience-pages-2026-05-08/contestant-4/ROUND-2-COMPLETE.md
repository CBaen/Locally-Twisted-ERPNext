## What changed in Round 2

**2. Standalone "Celebrations of Life" section (new Section 4)**

- Two invitation paragraphs — not promotional, not clinical
- Soft CTA: "Start a conversation" — calibrated to the emotional register
- This section cannot be transplanted to civic, corporate, or schools without feeling wrong. It passes the room-furniture test.

**3. Hero lede refined**
Reordered to give "celebrations of life" equal weight in the lede rather than trailing at the end of a list. The tone shift is subtle but correct: each occasion type "designed with care for the moment it represents."

### Schools — one copy tightening

The "School Colors" pillar body and service note body were sharpened from "No guessing, no surprises" to the specific claim: "university red, institutional navy — sourced to the school's official palette, not the catalog's nearest option." This is the precision the proxy called for in Loop 1; it was in the service note copy but not yet in the pillar body.

### Civic and corporate — held firm

No structural changes. The field confirmed corporate brand-color swatches and civic authority band as genuine differentiators. Polish only — no copy regressions introduced.
