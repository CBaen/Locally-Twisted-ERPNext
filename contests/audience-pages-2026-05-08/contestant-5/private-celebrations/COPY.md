## Story block

**Eyebrow:** How personal celebrations work differently

**H2:** The decor should feel like a gift, not a rental.

**Body paragraph 1:**
A wedding arch that took a month to plan shouldn't feel like a standard package. A 60th birthday arch for someone who's been the center of their family for six decades shouldn't look the same as the children's party arch across town. Private celebrations are personal — and the balloon decor is one of the few things that guests see, photograph, and remember before the celebration even begins.

**Body paragraph 2:**
The design conversation for a private event starts with what the host is imagining, not what's in a catalog. Color choices, balloon types, scale, and installation approach are all discussed before anything is quoted. The team has done 300+ birthday installs ranging from children's themed arches to milestone 50th and 60th arrangements that match the gravitas of the occasion. The work for weddings across the Wasatch Front includes organic half-arches, floral-accent installations, and ceremony backdrops at indoor and outdoor venues.

**Body paragraph 3:**
Delivery, setup, and teardown are included in private event quotes so the host can spend the morning getting ready, not managing vendors.

**H2:** Memorial and celebration-of-life events.

**Body paragraph 1:**

If you're planning a celebration-of-life event and considering balloon decor as part of the tribute, share the context when you reach out. The conversation will be handled accordingly.
