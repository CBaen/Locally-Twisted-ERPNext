{# --- 4. Story block (Stone) --------------------------------------- #}

What corporate work requires

Brand-safe means something specific.

A ribbon cutting for a bank branch has different stakes than a backyard birthday. The balloon arch is in the photos the PR team uses, the social posts the marketing director approves, and the local news coverage that follows. The colors have to match the brand standards document, the installation has to look polished in wide-angle photography, and the vendor has to be easy to work with through the internal approval chain.

Locally Twisted has supplied brand activations for KSL, KUTV, FOX13, and Megaplex — events where the install is photographed professionally and the brand is watching. The work for America First Credit Union and Zions Bank has to be conservative, precise, and finished before doors open. The FanX install has to hold for days of foot traffic. These are different problems with different execution requirements, and the company has done all of them.

Every corporate engagement starts with a quote that includes delivery, installation, and teardown. Standard vendor documentation available for purchase orders and AP routing.
