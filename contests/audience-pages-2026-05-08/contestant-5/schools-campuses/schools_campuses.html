What school events require

Spirit is specific. Schedules are real.

A graduation garland has to match the school's colors — not close, not approximately. Red that reads maroon in photos or blue that drifts toward teal breaks the visual discipline that student life offices and parents expect. School balloon work is the most color-critical category in the business, because the school's identity is attached to it.

The other constraint is the schedule. Back-to-school assemblies have a 7am install window before students arrive. Graduation setups happen in the morning while the venue is still being configured for the ceremony. Pep rally backdrops go up between lunch and the afternoon bell. The team understands that school event windows aren't flexible, and the install timeline is part of every quote conversation.

Locally Twisted has done graduation work, back-to-school stage installations, and campus events for University of Utah, Weber State, and St. Joseph's High School. The color-matching and schedule discipline aren't extras — they're how the job is done.

{# --- 6. School color note (Warm White, Stone rule) ---------------- #}

School colors aren't suggestions.

Color-matching for school events starts with the school's official palette, not the balloon catalog's nearest option. Share the school's color codes or reference photos and the team will source the closest match. For schools with very specific official colors — university red, institutional navy, etc. — the matching conversation happens before the quote is finalized, not after materials are sourced.

Custom color palettes are available for student-run events, prom, and end-of-year celebrations where the school wants something other than the standard palette. Color selections are confirmed before booking so there are no surprises on install day.
