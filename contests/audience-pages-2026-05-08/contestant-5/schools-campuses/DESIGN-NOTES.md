# Design Notes — Schools & Campuses

## Audience

Activity directors, athletic department coordinators, PTAs, college student life offices, graduation ceremony organizers. Buyer posture: spirit-driven, schedule-constrained, color-disciplined, family-friendly, often with tight budgets and tighter timelines.

## Page intent

This buyer has two central concerns:
1. Will the colors actually match our school palette?
2. Will the install be done before students arrive?

Every design decision on this page is answering those two questions, explicitly or implicitly. The roster is smaller than the other pages (only 3 named school clients vs. 26 civic or 30 corporate), so the design compensates by featuring those clients more prominently (Cormorant Garamond H2-scale treatment) and expanding proof through occasions and photography.

## Section decisions

### 1. Hero
Background: `school-back-to-school-stage.webp` — a large school stage display, the strongest single image of school-scale work. Immediately establishes that this isn't craft fair balloon twisting; it's professional school event production. Eyebrow lists the specific contexts: "Graduation · Back to school · Spirit events · Campus." H1 includes the key phrase "schools and campuses" so the buyer knows instantly they're in the right place. Lede calls out both core concerns (color-matched, schedule-disciplined) in 19 words.

### 2. Client proof band (Ink)
Only 3 named clients, so they get prominence rather than density. Each is displayed at H3 scale (Cormorant Garamond, large) with the context in brass-tinted uppercase below. This choice says "we know these relationships matter and we're not hiding them in a grid." A note below the grid adds contextual clients (Ogden City, Tree House Museum, Station Park) that serve school-age families — expanding the proof without inventing clients.

### 3. Photo proof
Four photos:
- `school-back-to-school-stage.webp` — large stage install
- `school-grad-garland.webp` — graduation ceremony garland
- `corporate-wsu-arch-bouquets.webp` — Weber State campus event (named client work)
- Parades/`Back to school stage display.png` — additional back-to-school stage proof from Odoo library

Two back-to-school stage photos is intentional: this is the highest-proof school format in the portfolio and worth showing twice at different angles.

### 4. Story block (Stone)
Two editorial paragraphs plus a credential close. First paragraph addresses color-matching with specificity ("Red that reads maroon... blue that drifts toward teal"). This is the exact language an activity director has thought about when a prior vendor got it wrong. Second paragraph addresses the schedule constraint explicitly: "7am install window," "between lunch and the afternoon bell." Third paragraph names the three clients again as credential close.

### 5. Occasions grid (Warm White)
Six cards covering the full school calendar: Graduation/Commencement, Back-to-School, Homecoming & Spirit, Prom & Formal, PTA/Community, and Athletic Milestones. Left-brass-border treatment (3px Brass, no top/right/bottom color border) — distinguishes these from the white cards on other pages and gives a slight school-colors-discipline visual signal without using actual school colors. 3-column desktop, 2-column tablet, 1-column mobile = 3+3 (no orphans).

### 6. School color note (Warm White, Stone rule)
The most school-specific section — addresses exactly the color-matching concern in practical detail: share color codes or reference photos, matching conversation happens before quote finalization, custom palettes available for student events. This is operational information that reduces the buyer's anxiety and removes an objection without requiring a phone call.

### 7. Icon bar (Navy)
Four icons: school-spirit, utah-rooted, event-stage, professional. All chosen for school-specific relevance. "Schedule Disciplined" note under professional directly calls out the install timing concern.

### 8. CTA (Ink)
"Tell us about your school event." — first-person invitation. Body copy mentions the three things the buyer needs to share (event, date, school colors) — removes friction by telling them exactly what information to bring.

## Color pattern
Ink (proof band) → Warm White (photos) → Stone (story) → Warm White (occasions + color note) → Navy (icon bar) → Ink (CTA)

## Distinct school-only elements
- Large-type named client display (vs. grid density on other pages — compensates for smaller roster)
- Occasions grid with brass left-border cards (school calendar coverage)
- School color discipline section (unique to this page)
- Eyebrow/lede explicitly naming both core buyer concerns
- "Between lunch and the afternoon bell" specificity in story copy
