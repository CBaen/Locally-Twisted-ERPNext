## Story block

**Eyebrow:** What civic work looks like

**H2:** Public events have a different pressure.

**Body paragraph 1:**
A city ribbon cutting or a Pride parade isn't a corporate dinner where you control the guest list. The balloon work is photographed by the public, shared across social channels before the event is over, and remembered by the whole community — not just attendees. The install has to read from a distance, hold through weather, and look right in the photos that end up in municipal newsletters, local news coverage, and organizer reports.

**Body paragraph 2:**
Locally Twisted has done this work across eight Wasatch Front municipalities, for Pride organizers with visibility expectations, and for chambers of commerce where the install is the first thing people photograph when they arrive. The work knows the context. Quotes for civic events include delivery, install, and teardown as standard — coordinators don't chase a vendor at the end of the day.

**Body paragraph 3:**
Every civic engagement starts with a free quote scoped to the event. Permits, outdoor duration, and weather exposure are part of the conversation, not afterthoughts.

## Service note

**H2:** What's included in a civic quote

**Cards:**

**Site visit & measurement**
For large outdoor installs, the team walks the site before quote finalization. Parade routes and public venues have real constraints that matter to the design.

**Delivery, install & teardown**
Civic quotes include setup and strike as standard. Coordinators aren't responsible for retrieving hardware or managing vendor cleanup at the end of an event.

**Outdoor durability planning**
Utah summers, wind, and direct sun affect balloons differently than climate-controlled venues. Weather exposure and event duration are part of every outdoor civic quote.

**Custom color matching**
City colors, Pride flags, chamber branding, and event-specific palettes. Balloon color choices are a design conversation, not a catalog constraint.

**Multi-location events**
Parade routes, multi-stage events, and events with installations at several points are within scope. Share the event map and the team will quote accordingly.

**Vendor documentation**
Insurance certificates and vendor paperwork for public event permit compliance. Ask at the time of inquiry.
