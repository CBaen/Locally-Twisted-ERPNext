# Contestant 5 — Audience Pages Suite

## Concept: The Proof-First Buyer Suite

**Signature:** Each page opens by naming the buyer's world — their context, their pressure, their language — and then puts real client work in front of them immediately. Not a welcome mat. A credential check.

The four pages share a visual grammar but diverge hard in tone, proof selection, and copy register:

- **Civic & Community** — civic authority mode. Named city clients, Pride partnerships, public-event scale. The coordinator comparing two vendors sees a company that already knows how Utah public events work.
- **Corporate Events** — professional restraint. Named brands from broadcast, banking, food, and entertainment sectors. The marketing director who needs AP invoicing and brand-safe execution sees a company that already works with their peers.
- **Schools & Campuses** — spirit meets schedule discipline. UofU, Weber State, St. Joseph's, graduation install proof. The activity director sees a company that understands school color discipline and event-day punctuality.
- **Private Celebrations** — milestone warmth. No named clients (privacy). Category-level proof, testimonial texture, and elevated aesthetic. The wedding planner or birthday parent sees a company that treats personal moments as seriously as corporate ones.

## Structural grammar (shared across all four)

1. **Hero** — compact, named-buyer eyebrow, H1 that owns the space, one-line lede. No CTA crowding.
2. **Client proof band** — dark authority band (navy/ink), named clients in a disciplined grid or crawl. Immediate credibility anchor.
3. **Photo proof section** — 3–4 real installed-work images with context captions (install type, not client name).
4. **Story/case study block** — one narrative paragraph about what this buyer needs and how LT delivers it. Editorial register.
5. **Service note** — audience-specific service clarifications (what's included, how quotes work, what's custom vs. ready-to-order).
6. **Icon proof bar** — brass-line icons from the brand suite, 3–4 relevant to this buyer.
7. **CTA section** — navy or ink band, invitation copy (not pressure), contact link.

## Design discipline

- Hero contract honored: 220px mobile / 250px tablet / 280px desktop, strict.
- No light-blue, blush, or pastel anywhere.
- No `!important`. No new global CSS files. Page-scoped `<style>` blocks only.
- No two adjacent full-width colored bands.
- All clients from the approved roster only.
- All photo references point at real files (optimized portfolio tree + Odoo source tree).
- Cormorant Garamond headings, Lato body/UI throughout.
- Container modes declared per style guide: `band`, `fullbleed`, `contained`.
