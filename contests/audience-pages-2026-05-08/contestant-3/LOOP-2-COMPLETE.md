# Loop 2 Complete — Contestant 3 (Made For You / Anxiety-First)

## What the proxy found

**Loop 2-1 (convergence-survival test):** The civic page's anxiety-first architecture was the quietest of the four. The civic coordinator's actual fear — professional embarrassment at a public-facing event — was close to the surface in the copy but hadn't been named at the H1 level the way corporate named its three fears. The architecture was three-and-a-half pages, not four.

**Loop 2-2 (proof-vs-promise integrity test):** Corporate's three-part H1 ("On-brand. On-time. Invoice-ready.") makes a three-part promise. "On-brand" and "Invoice-ready" had systematic proof. "On-time" was promised in the H1 and implied in the process steps but not explicitly stated as a commitment with a consequence — the promise outran the evidence.

## What changed

### Civic H1 — anxiety named at the top

Old H1: "Balloon decor built for Utah's public stage."

New H1: **"Photographed. Accountable. On record."**

Three words, three fears named before a single credential appears. "Photographed" names the visual accountability of public-event work — the backdrop for a thousand photos. "Accountable" names the professional-embarrassment fear directly: the coordinator is on the hook to her director, her city council, or the cameras. "On record" names the audit/documentation stakes that civic work carries in ways private events don't. The lede was extended one phrase to carry through: "reflect well on the people who planned them."

This completes the anxiety-first architecture across all four pages. A reviewer can now point to the naming move on every page without being told where to look.

### Corporate process step 03 — "on-time" proof closed

Old title/body: "Installed, documented, struck" / implied on-time delivery.

New title: **"On-time, documented, struck clean"** — the promise from the H1 now appears as a section title in the proof structure.

New body adds the explicit commitment mechanism: "We confirm arrival window before your event and hold to it... If anything changes, you hear from us first — not your venue coordinator." That last sentence names the specific failure mode the buyer fears (finding out late, from someone else) and forecloses it. The H1 promise now has a proof answer in the process section.
