# Proxy Loop 2-1 — Contestant 3 (Made For You / Anxiety-First)

## Lens: The convergence-survival test

Both "Made For You" suites (C3 and C8) cleared the memorial test in Round 2. The
field has now converged on memorial competence, which means the memorial section
is no longer the differentiator — it's the entry fee. The differentiator is now
your other three pages.

Here's where C3's signature is most distinct from C8's: C8's mechanism is
"named-promise architecture" — each page makes a specific, nameable commitment that
the buyer can hold LT to. C3's mechanism is "anxiety-first" — each page names the
buyer's fear before the first credential appears.

On corporate, this shows up cleanly with "On-brand. On-time. Invoice-ready." as the
H1 — that's naming the three fears before any client roster or case study. On
schools, it shows up in the schedule anxiety copy. But the civic page's anxiety
naming is the quietest of the four — and that's the page where the fear is arguably
the most specific and least often named in competing vendor materials.

What is the civic coordinator's actual fear? Not "will the balloons look good" —
that's table stakes. It's more like: "will this vendor make me look bad in front of
my director, my city council, or the cameras?" The fear is professional embarrassment
at a public-facing event. That specific fear is close to the surface in your civic
copy but hasn't been named directly the way the corporate page names its fears.

Compare: corporate H1 = "On-brand. On-time. Invoice-ready." Those three words name
three fears. What would the equivalent three-word structure look like for civic? It
might be something like "Photographed. Permitted. Professional." or something that
names the accountability stakes of public-event work. You don't have to build it
now — but if you did, C3's anxiety-first architecture would be complete across all
four pages, not three and a half.

What's working: the decision to give KJSCOTT one structural home and remove it from
testimonials entirely is the most disciplined KJSCOTT deployment in the field.
Full weight in one place beats diluted presence in two.
