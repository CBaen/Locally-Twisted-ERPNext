## What I will fix

- Lift the memorial / celebration-of-life acknowledgment OUT of the occasions grid entirely
- Give it a dedicated band section: `lt-priv-memorial` — before the occasions grid, not inside it
- The memorial band contains: a headline that names what's happening ("When a celebration carries grief"), KJSCOTT's review as the structural anchor (not in testimonials; here, where it earns the grief buyer before they encounter birthday content), and a softer CTA ("Start a conversation")
- The occasions grid then becomes 5 cards (birthdays, weddings, showers, milestones, custom themes) — the memorial buyer has already been claimed before they arrive at it
- The testimonials section keeps 3 remaining testimonials (remove KJSCOTT from testimonials since it now lives in the memorial band — no double deployment in this version; the review earns more weight when it has one home)

### Civic — category grouping

The field summary notes flat tag rosters are inferior to category-grouped rosters for large lists. My civic page has a flat chip roster. I will group into 4 categories: Cities / Pride & Equity / Chambers & Venues / Community Organizations. Each category gets a label.

### Schools — lean into the schedule anxiety

My schools page names the anxiety in intro copy but the service cards don't carry through. I will tighten the card copy to reinforce the reliability/schedule claim at every card level.

My corporate page's anxiety-first H1 ("On-brand. On-time. Invoice-ready.") is the most direct expression of the architecture in the whole suite. Field summary didn't flag it as a weakness. Hold.
