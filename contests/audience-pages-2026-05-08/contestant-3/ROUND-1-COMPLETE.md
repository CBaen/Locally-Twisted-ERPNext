# Round 1 Complete — Contestant 3

## Concept: "Made For You"

Four distinct audience pages built around the specific buyer's anxieties, vocabulary, and decision criteria. Each page's H1, intro, proof selection, and CTA is optimized for one audience — a civic coordinator reading the corporate page would feel like a guest in the wrong room. The suite shares structural discipline and quiet confidence voice, but calibrates per audience: Americana civic authority, functional corporate precision, schedule-aware school reliability, and emotionally present private celebration warmth. Every image is contextually matched; every client name is from the real roster; every proof claim is category-level or specifically attributed.
