Why Locally Twisted for civic work

Public events need installations that hold up — and stand out.

A city ribbon cutting, a Pride parade float, a county fair entrance, a chamber gala — each one is a public statement. The balloon work isn't decoration; it's the backdrop for a thousand photos, the landmark attendees orient around, the visual proof that the event was worth attending.

Since 1998, Locally Twisted has installed civic-scale balloon work across the Wasatch Front: parades that travel five miles, stages that face thousands, and entrances that welcome every kind of community. Every install is engineered for outdoor conditions, built to photograph, and struck cleanly after.
