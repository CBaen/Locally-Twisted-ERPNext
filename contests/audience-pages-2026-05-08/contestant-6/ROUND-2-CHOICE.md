## Assessment

The field summary confirms this is the differentiator worth keeping. No other contestant matched this architecture.

2. Use KJSCOTT's words in a structural position that earns trust
3. Give memorial work a container commensurate with its weight

I have (3). I partially have (2) in design notes but not in the HTML. I don't have (1).
