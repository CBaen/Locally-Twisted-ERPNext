## The perspective: Procurement skeptic (corporate page)

You've done the AP section. Let's go deeper. Read your corporate page as a marketing director at a mid-size company — someone who's been burned before by a vendor that was enthusiastic during quoting and chaotic on install day.

She's looking for two things your AP note doesn't yet answer:

First: **what does communication look like during the process?** Her fear isn't only "can I get an invoice." It's "will I be chasing this vendor for a confirmation the week before the event?" The AP note handles the paper trail. Does the page handle the human reliability?

Second: **what happens if something goes wrong on the day?** Corporate events have stakeholders watching. If a column falls over at the grand opening, who do you call?

Your service note handles the process steps. Your AP note handles the documentation. But the moment between "install is complete" and "event ends" — who is on site and what's their direct number?

**What to try:** Read your corporate CTA: "Share the venue, the color system, and the load-in window." That's a strong closing. But it's all about what LT needs from the buyer. Is there a line that addresses what the buyer can count on from LT once the quote is accepted — not the invoice process, but the human availability during the event?

A busy marketing director doesn't want to feel like she submitted her specs into a system. She wants to feel like there's a person on the other end. Does your page earn that?
