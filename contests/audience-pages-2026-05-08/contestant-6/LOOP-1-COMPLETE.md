# Loop 1 Complete — Contestant 6: "Buyer-Scoped Authority"

## What the proxy named, and how I responded

### Proxy 1-1 — Corporate page: human availability

**The feedback.** The AP note handles the paper trail. It does not handle what a marketing director burned by chaotic vendors actually fears: "Will I be chasing this vendor for a confirmation the week before?" And "if something goes wrong on install day, who do I call?"

The CTA closing — "Share the venue, the color system, and the load-in window" — asks for what LT needs from the buyer. It doesn't offer what the buyer can count on from LT.

**What I adjusted.** The trust aside now has two paragraphs. The first (unchanged) handles AP documentation. The second is new — brief, direct, personal:

> Once the quote is accepted, you'll have a direct contact — not a ticket system. For install-day events, Jeff is on site through strike. If something needs adjusting after setup, you call the number that answers.

The two paragraphs are visually separated by a thin rule inside the aside. The heading changed from "Clean invoicing for your AP team" to "Clean invoicing. A real person on event day." — which names both things the section now delivers.

**Where I held firm.** The AP note language is unchanged. It's the most operationally specific treatment of corporate procurement in the competition. The proxy confirmed that. I didn't dilute it to make room for warmth — I added warmth alongside it.

---

### Proxy 1-2 — Private celebrations page: pacing and breath

**The feedback.** Four occasion blocks with full prose and 3-photo clusters back to back can read as thoroughness or as overwhelming. The question was: where's the exhale? Does a buyer scrolling through four substantial blocks lose the thread before reaching the CTA?

**What I adjusted.** A thin `<hr>` rule (`lt-private-moment__rule`) now appears between occasion blocks — not before the first, not after the last. It's rendered at 50% opacity, color `var(--lt-stone)`, no height, no decoration. It is a visual breath, not a design statement. The moment blocks themselves are unchanged — each still carries full prose, 3 photos, and a per-occasion CTA link.

**Where I held firm.** The four-occasion architecture stays. The proxy was explicit: "The occasions themselves are right. The question is pacing." Collapsing to cards would destroy what makes this page a genuine investment in each occasion type — including the memorial/celebration-of-life section that the proxy called out as the most distinctive structural move in the field. A thin rule between blocks is the minimum intervention that creates rhythm without compromising depth.

---

## Net state

Two targeted edits. Both responsive to the proxy's named concern. Neither compromises the concept's core posture: buyers who know their domain deserve a page that knows it too.

The Buyer-Scoped Authority concept holds.
