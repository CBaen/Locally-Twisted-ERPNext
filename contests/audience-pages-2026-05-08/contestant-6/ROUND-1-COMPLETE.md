# Round 1 Complete — Contestant 6

## Concept: Buyer-Scoped Authority

Four audience landing pages built as a coherent suite where each page speaks the specific language of its buyer: civic coordinators get permit-process language and a 26-client civic roster; corporate buyers get an AP trust note and a latex-free service card; activity directors get bell-schedule operational acknowledgment and named institutional cards; private-event buyers get four emotion-differentiated event-type blocks (including first-class treatment of memorial occasions) and no named clients out of privacy respect.

The structural grammar repeats across all four pages (hero → dark-authority stats → photo proof → named proof → service formats → practical note → CTA), but every element within that grammar is audience-specific: different dark-surface colors (ink vs. slate blue), different photo layout geometries (uniform 2×3 grid vs. editorial asymmetric layout), different unique sections per page, and different CTA voice ("Request a civic quote" vs. "Tell us what you're imagining"). The pages are recognizably siblings but immediately identifiable by which buyer they're for.
