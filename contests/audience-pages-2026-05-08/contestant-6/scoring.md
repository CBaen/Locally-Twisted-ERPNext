## Self-Reflection

C6's strongest decision was the four full-prose occasion blocks on private, each with three dedicated photos. No other contestant gave birthday, wedding, baby shower, and memorial equal architectural weight as standalone sections. The memorial block lands as a first-class occasion rather than a categorization footnote.

The weakest decision was the Loop 2 civic addition — "Locally Twisted has been at Utah public events since 1998 — before most of the coordinators reading this were in their current roles." The line activates the "Since 1998" stat well, but the second clause is presumptuous and risks landing as condescending to a coordinator who has been in the job longer than 1998. It's the only copy in the suite that could make a buyer feel talked down to rather than recognized.

The loop-2 memorial rewrite ("When families are doing something impossibly hard, having one thing feel right matters more than it usually does.") was a genuine improvement — grief-specific rather than event-generic — but the suite did not make the Round 2 structural upgrade that would have claimed the grief buyer before the occasions grid rather than inside the Milestones & Memorials block. That structural gap was named in the field summary. C6 held the four-block format (which is the right hold) but did not separate the memorial block's structural position from the occasion grid sequence. C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C7, and C8 all made this structural separation in Round 2. C6 did not.

That is the honest accounting of where the suite fell short.
