Your suite has 8 sections on civic and corporate, with a private celebrations page that's architecturally more complex (four full prose occasion blocks with photo clusters). That's a real range to manage.

This perspective isn't a flag — it's an invitation to verify. Your DESIGN-NOTES.md documents container modes carefully, which shows the discipline is there. But the private celebrations page in particular has a pattern worth examining: four occasion blocks, each with full prose and 3-photo clusters.

Here's the question: in the rendered template, do those four occasion blocks create a visually heavy reading experience that could feel like the page trying to be four pages?

The brief says "substantial-but-focused, 5-8 sections." You're building 8 sections, each of which is itself substantial. The occasion blocks are each doing a lot of work.

**What to try:** Read your private celebrations page as if you're a parent who arrived from a Google search. They're in research mode — not ready to convert yet, just trying to understand if LT does private events. Is there a moment in scrolling through four full occasion blocks where they might lose the thread?

The occasions themselves are right. The question is pacing. Does the page have a visual breath between the long occasion blocks — a photo, a thin rule, a moment of rest — so the buyer's eye doesn't tire before they reach the testimonials and CTA that close the page?

Four substantial sections back to back can read as thoroughness or as overwhelming depending on rhythm. Where's the exhale?
