# Design Notes — Schools & Campuses

## Audience and buyer posture
Activity directors, athletic department staff, PTAs, college student life coordinators, graduation organizers. This buyer is schedule-constrained more than any other audience — their event is locked to the school calendar, the gym is booked, the AV team is coming at 8am. They need: proof that LT understands school logistics, evidence of real school client history, color-matching confidence, and a frictionless quote process.

## Structural choices

### Hero
- Background: `school-back-to-school-stage.webp` — the most school-specific, stage-scale photo in the optimized library. Shows balloon decor at the kind of scale an activity director would recognize as "this is for our auditorium."
- H1: "School spirit, on your colors." — the phrase "school spirit" is the exact language activity directors use. "On your colors" promises color accuracy without using "Pantone" jargon.
- Eyebrow scopes the page: "Schools & Campuses" — distinct from civic and corporate.

### Stats authority band (slate blue, section 2)
Chose slate blue (not ink) for the stats band on this page. It's a lighter-feeling dark ground than ink — appropriate for an audience that's family-facing and K-12-inclusive. Still premium, still authoritative, but not as corporate-severe.

Four stats mapped to school-buyer concerns:
- "On-Spec" color matching — answers the "can you match our colors?" question first
- "Grad to Spirit" — names the spectrum of events explicitly
- "Load-In & Out" — the logistics answer, in the stats band where buyers scan early
- "Family-Safe" — answers the community/parent expectation

### Photo proof grid (2×3, section 3)
Six photos all from school contexts:
1. `school-back-to-school-stage.webp` — Weber State, back-to-school stage (same as hero, repeated for the grid is intentional — it's the strongest school proof)
2. `school-grad-garland.webp` — graduation context, garland
3. `corporate-wsu-arch-bouquets.webp` — WSU branded arch (the WSU colors read immediately)
4–5. Odoo back-to-school display photos (different angles of the same WSU install — shows depth of work)
6. UofU football — athletic event, University of Utah context

The brief notes the school roster is short. Rather than padding with weak clients, the photo grid serves as implicit proof — multiple angles of the same real WSU install show depth of relationship, not breadth of client count.

### Named institutions (navy cards, section 4)
Three institutions presented as cards, not a flat list. Each card shows:
- Institution type (high school / university)
- Name (large, Cormorant)
- Context (what kind of work)

This elevated treatment is appropriate given the small client count. Cards signal "we know these clients deeply" better than a generic list. The section copy directly acknowledges the focused roster ("Locally Twisted builds deep, returning relationships") — turning a potential weakness into a differentiator.

### Event type cards (section 5)
Four event types, each with client attribution where available:
- Graduation Ceremonies
- Back-to-School Events (Weber State named as returning client)
- Athletic Events & Pep Assemblies
- Campus & Classroom Events

Two-column desktop grid, full-width mobile.

### Practical scheduling note (sandstone, section 6)
The `lt-school-note` section is the school page's most audience-specific prose. It directly addresses "bell schedules, custodial windows, and athletic department access requirements" — language that only makes sense if you've done school events. This section earns credibility by proving institutional knowledge, not just event experience.

### CTA (navy, section 7)
Chose navy for the closing CTA — same as the institutions section above, giving the page a visual rhythm. The CTA is the only place where "graduation season" and "homecoming" appear explicitly — keeping school-specific vocabulary in the closing anchor.

## What makes this page feel specifically school
1. "Bell schedules" appears by name in the scheduling note
2. The client cards use "University" and "High School" type labels
3. Photos: 4 of 6 are WSU/UofU context
4. Stats include "Family-Safe" — no other audience page has this pillar
5. "School spirit" in the H1 — exact buyer vocabulary
6. WSU named as a "returning client" in the event card descriptions
