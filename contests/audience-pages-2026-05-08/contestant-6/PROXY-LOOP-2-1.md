# Proxy Loop 2-1 — Contestant 6 (Buyer-Scoped Authority)

## Lens: The unsafe choice

You made the right structural choice in Round 2 by not collapsing the four-prose
occasion blocks to cards. That was the safe hold. Here's the unsafe question: is
there a moment inside those four blocks where the copy plays it too safe?

Read the birthday block. Then read the wedding block. Then the baby showers block.
Then Milestones & Memorials. The first three blocks are describing occasions with
accurate, tasteful copy. The fourth — Milestones & Memorials — is the only block
where the copy is doing something fundamentally different: it's speaking to a buyer
who may be in grief.

The birthday copy says what the balloon work is. The memorial copy should say what
the balloon work does for someone who is grieving. There's a distinction there. "The
decor sets the tone for the room, gives people something to gather around, and makes
the tribute feel finished" is close — "makes the tribute feel finished" is a real
insight. But the rest of that sentence ("sets the tone for the room, gives people
something to gather around") could appear on any event planning page. It's accurate;
it's not yet grief-specific.

The unsafe version of the memorial copy would name something that only happens at a
memorial — something that a birthday balloon vendor would never say because they'd
have no reason to. Something like: "When families are doing something impossibly
hard, having one thing feel right matters more than it usually does." That sentence
could only appear on a memorial page. It proves the writer thought specifically about
the grief buyer, not just about the event category.

You don't have to build that sentence today. But recognizing that "memorial-specific"
and "event-appropriate" aren't the same thing will make the revision, if you do it,
sharper.

What's working: naming Jeff specifically in the extended memorial description — "Jeff
and the team have walked families through this" — is the most humanizing move in the
memorial section of any suite. Institutional claims don't earn grief buyer trust.
Personal names do.
