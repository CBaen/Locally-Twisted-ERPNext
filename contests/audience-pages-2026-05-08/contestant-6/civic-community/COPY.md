## Process Note

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**Heading:** Civic events move fast. The quote process doesn't have to.

**Body:** Public events come with permits, timing constraints, and committee sign-offs. The quote is the first step — and it's a conversation, not a form submission into a void.

**Steps:**

1. **Tell us the event.** Date, venue, crowd size, and what you're trying to do visually. A site map or venue layout helps if you have one.

2. **We scope the install.** We'll ask about wind exposure, permit requirements, load-in window, and power access if relevant.

3. **Quote, confirm, install, and strike.** Your crew doesn't touch the decor — setup and takedown are included.
