How it works

Civic events move fast. The quote process doesn't have to.

Public events come with permits, timing constraints, and committee sign-offs. The quote is the first step — and it's a conversation, not a form submission into a void.

  1. 1 Tell us the event. Date, venue, crowd size, and what you're trying to do visually. A site map or venue layout helps if you have one.
  2. 2 We scope the install. We'll ask about wind exposure, permit requirements, load-in window, and power access if relevant.
  3. 3 Quote, confirm, install, and strike. Your crew doesn't touch the decor — setup and takedown are included.
