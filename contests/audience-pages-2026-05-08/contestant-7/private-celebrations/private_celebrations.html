{# --- 7. SERVICE NOTE (band) --- #}

For families, couples, and celebration planners

How the process works for private clients

Start with what you're imagining — the event type, approximate date, and a sense of the palette or style. A quote is returned that includes materials, delivery, setup, and teardown. Delivery times are coordinated so the decor is in place before the first guest arrives. For celebrations of life and memorials, the team communicates directly and quietly — the process doesn't add to the weight of the occasion. Photography is yours to share or keep private; Locally Twisted does not post client event photos without permission.
