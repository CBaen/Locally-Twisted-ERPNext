For event coordinators and procurement teams

How the process works for civic clients

Quote requests go directly to the Locally Twisted team — no agency layers. Civic and government clients can use purchase orders; vendor forms and W-9 are available on request. Installations are coordinated with your venue or event operations contact. Teardown and strike are included unless the event scope requires otherwise. For parade route work, clearance dimensions and installation timing are confirmed before any materials are staged.
