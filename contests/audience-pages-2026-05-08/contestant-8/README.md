# Contestant 8 — Suite Concept: "Made For You"

## Signature

Each page opens with a hero that *names the buyer* in its H1 — not a generic "event decor"
claim, but a sentence the buyer could have written themselves. The civic coordinator sees
"Civic-scale balloon decor for Utah cities, counties, and community organizations." The
corporate buyer sees language about brand-safe, repeatable, AP-friendly installs.

The structural spine across all four pages:

1. **Compact hero** — buyer-named, authority-grounded, with one CTA
2. **Client proof band** — named real clients in a dark authority band (no logos, text-weight names)
3. **Installed-work photo row** — 2–3 real portfolio images with minimal labeling
4. **Case study / story block** — one named client or event type with concrete specifics
5. **Service notes** — what LT actually does for this audience (no inventions)
6. **Proof icons** — 4 brass icons from the brand system
7. **CTA section** — deep navy or warm white, audience-specific invite

No page clones another. Each chooses different photos, different client angles, different copy frames.

## Page-by-page summary

### `/civic-community`
Buyer: city events coordinators, Pride organizers, chambers, county events.
Proof: SLC Pride, 12+ Utah cities (Ogden, Sandy, Herriman, Kearns, Hooper, Syracuse, West Point, Clinton), SLC County, UDOT, Gallivan Center.
Hero image: Pride rainbow columns (Odoo library, `seasonal-pride-columns.webp` in portfolio).
Signature move: the "12 Utah cities" number grounds the roster without inventing facts.

### `/corporate-events`
Buyer: marketing teams, brand activations, broadcasters, banks, corporate parties.
Proof: KSL, KUTV, FOX13, Ancestry, Zions Bank, America First CU, Utah Jazz, Fidelity, Morgan Stanley, Chick-Fil-A, Texas Roadhouse, IHC, Young Automotive.
Hero image: `corporate-logo-arch.webp` (production portfolio).
Signature move: "Brand-safe. Repeatable. On your colors." — three words that a marketing director actually thinks in.

### `/schools-campuses`
Buyer: activity directors, athletic departments, PTAs, college student life, graduation organizers.
Proof: University of Utah, Weber State University, St. Joseph's High School.
Hero image: `school-back-to-school-stage.webp` + `school-grad-garland.webp` + `corporate-wsu-arch-bouquets.webp`.
Signature move: the "School Colors, Respected" block — a named promise that LT matches school colors exactly, backed by WSU and UofU examples.

### `/private-celebrations`
Buyer: birthday parents, wedding planners, baby shower hosts, milestone families.
Proof: Category-level (300+ birthdays, Wasatch Front weddings, memorials).
Hero image: `wedding-organic-half-arch.webp`.
Signature move: the memorial/celebration-of-life acknowledgment — few balloon companies name this with the dignity it deserves. One review quote (KJSCOTT) anchors the section.
