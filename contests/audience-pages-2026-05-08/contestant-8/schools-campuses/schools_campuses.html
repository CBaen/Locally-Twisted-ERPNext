School Colors, Respected

Not close enough. Exactly right.

When Weber State University needed balloon decor for a campus event, the request was specific: Weber State purple and white, at entrance arch and bouquet scale. Not a generic purple — Weber State purple. The University of Utah has had the same expectation. So does every activity director working in school colors: the decor should look like it belongs to the school, not like it was rented from a generic vendor.

That's the approach here. Bring the school colors, or point at the brand guide — LT sources balloon latex in the palette that matches. Installations reflect the school, not the balloon company.

What School Events Need

School events run on strict timing and family-appropriate standards. These are the specifics.

Graduation Ceremonies

Balloon garlands, arches, and column pairs for stage, entrance, and photo-moment locations. School colors matched. Installed before ceremony start.

Spirit Events & Homecoming

School-spirit arches and backdrops for pep rallies, homecoming, spirit weeks, and athletic events. Installed and struck to fit the school's schedule.

Back-to-School & Campus Welcome

Stage fills, entrance pieces, and photo-moment backdrops for orientation days, back-to-school kickoffs, and student life events at K-12 and university scale.

PTA & Family Events

Family-friendly carnival arches, classroom decorations, and bouquet arrangements for school fairs, fundraisers, and PTA-organized events.
