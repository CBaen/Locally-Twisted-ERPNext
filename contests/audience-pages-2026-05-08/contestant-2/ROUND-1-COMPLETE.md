# Round 1 Complete — Contestant 2

## Concept name
**"The Right Room"**

## One-paragraph summary
Four audience pages built on the principle that the buyer needs to feel recognized before they finish reading the first section — not just told they're in the right place, but shown it through the image, the hero line, the exact client names they'll recognize, and copy that speaks in their professional language. Each page uses the same structural DNA (hero, proof, stories, dark authority band, service notes, gallery, CTA) but varies card treatment, emotional register, roster format, and dark band placement so no two pages feel like copies of each other. Civic gets operational authority and named city proof. Corporate gets AP-language and industry-sorted client tables. Schools gets honest depth-over-breadth roster framing and budget acknowledgment. Private gets editorial headlines for each celebration type, preserved privacy, and real customer voice from Google reviews — with one section specifically written for the person planning a memorial who needs to feel the company will handle it with care.

## One sentence per page

**Civic & Community (/civic-community):** Proves LT has done city-scale work — not just balloon work at city events — through named client tables sorted by civic sector (Cities, Pride/Equality, Chambers, Community Venues) and proof stories anchored to SLC Pride, Gallivan Center, and Ogden City.

**Corporate Events (/corporate-events):** Speaks the B2B language procurement and marketing teams actually use (load-in window, AP invoicing, COI, color system, itemized invoice) and displays 30+ clients sorted across five industry verticals so any corporate buyer spots their sector.

**Schools & Campuses (/schools-campuses):** Turns the intentionally short school roster into a trust signal ("deep relationships, not a long shallow list"), names the school-specific logistics every activity director worries about (custodial schedules, budget ceilings, school-exact color matching), and includes an 8-chip event-type matrix covering every moment of the school year.

**Private Celebrations (/private-celebrations):** Builds proof without named clients through four celebration-type panels with editorial headlines calibrated to each buyer's emotional state — including a specifically care-written section for celebrations of life — supported by real verified Google reviews attributed by first name and event type only.
