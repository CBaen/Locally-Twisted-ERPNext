# Peer Scoring — Contestant 2 (The Right Room)

Scored on three dimensions, 1–10 each:
1. **Audience fit** — does each page feel made for its buyer?
2. **Proof density** — does the real roster + real photo library carry believable weight?
3. **Style discipline** — does the suite obey the style guide while still bringing craft?

---

## Contestant 1 — Audience Authority

- Audience fit: 9/10. The operational specificity is real throughout. The civic page names COI, W-9, PO/net-30 by their exact Utah procurement names — a Sandy City coordinator reads that and recognizes her own process. The corporate crawl is structurally honest: you cannot fake 30 client names scrolling past. The schools page names "gyms are in use until the hour before" which is a lived constraint, not a guess. The private page earns its emotional warmth without slipping into cute. The Round 2 memorial addition — KJSCOTT in structural position before the category grid with a direct-address heading — closes the one gap from Round 1 and meets all three memorial test conditions simultaneously. The buyers feel seen across the board.
- Proof density: 9/10. The 26-client civic grid with categories, the 30-name corporate crawl, three named school clients with specific event notes, and real Google review excerpts on private all deploy proof from the actual roster. The IHC and Odoo library image references are documented for implementation rather than invented. The Loop 2 note about the crawl proving scale but not color precision is an honest self-assessment that shows the contestant knows exactly where the gap is.
- Style discipline: 8/10. The corporate scrolling crawl was identified in the field summary as "a structural choice, not a copy choice" and it's the clearest visual differentiator in the suite. Hero contract documented correctly, no adjacent same-color fullbleed bands, container modes declared, no forbidden fonts. Minor: the "two adjacent dark sections" concern on the private page (hero-to-memorial-entry both described as dark, though the slate memorial band is distinct enough from the image hero that it reads as a visual step rather than a violation). On-voice throughout — "Tell us about your event" not "Book now".
- **Total: 26/30**
- Notes: The Loop 2 documentation for both loops was particularly good — the maintainer protection note in civic DESIGN-NOTES.md naming exactly which terms must not be paraphrased is something the winning page needs and the contestant got there independently.

## Self-Reflection — Where My Work is Weakest Relative to the Field

Reading the field honestly, the card-border-direction posture system is my strongest differentiator and the most genuinely invisible-to-the-buyer design decision in the contest. The celebration-of-life panel copy ("It is not a department. It is part of the work.") earned the highest specific Proxy praise in the field.

The weakest area relative to peers is **civic proof density**. My civic client table is grouped into four categories (which was the right move, and I did it from Round 1), but the field summary confirms that C1's vendor-documentation section and C5's five civic category groupings with work-type parentheticals go deeper into the operational specificity that a Sandy City events coordinator actually uses. My Round 2 civic procurement cards (invoicing terms, COI availability, documented delivery windows, permit-ready specs) close part of that gap, but C5's parenthetical work-type context on each civic category grouping is a level of proof granularity I didn't reach.

The second gap is **schools specificity**. C5's story block copy ("Red that reads maroon... blue that drifts toward teal") and C8's "Not close enough. Exactly right." are both more specific and more quotable than my trust-pillar language on the schools page, even after the Round 2 tightening pass. The "university crimson, institutional navy" specificity in the Round 2 pillars is better, but I didn't land a standalone named section for color-matching the way C8 did with "School Colors, Respected."

The corporate page is the one where I'm most competitive with the top peers. The industry-sector proof table and the AP/process bar are strong. The private page KJSCOTT deployment in structural position before the celebration-type panels is correctly executed. But the civic and schools pages would need another round of the kind of operational specificity that C1, C5, and C7 brought.
