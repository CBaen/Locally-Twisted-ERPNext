## Case story

**Eyebrow:** Proof of Work

**H2:** SLC Pride, Ogden Pride, and public events that Utah photographs.

**Context tag:** Utah's largest annual Pride celebration

**Body paragraph 1:**
Civic events are photographed. By press. By attendees. By city social media
accounts. The decor becomes the image — and that image reflects on the event
organizer.

**Body paragraph 2:**
Locally Twisted has served SLC Pride, the Pride Center, Equality Utah, and the
LGBT Chamber of Utah, alongside 8+ city governments across the Wasatch Front.
The installations are engineered for outdoor durability: full-day anchoring,
weather-tolerant materials, and a clean strike at the end so the venue is left
as it was found.

**Body paragraph 3:**
The design centers on the event's color story — whether that's the Progress Flag
palette for a Pride celebration, city brand colors for a ribbon cutting, or a
neutral civic palette for a public community event.
