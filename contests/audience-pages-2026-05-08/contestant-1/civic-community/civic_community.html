{# 3. Case story — band #}

Proof of Work

SLC Pride, Ogden Pride, and public events that Utah photographs.

{{ case_story.context }}

Civic events are photographed. By press. By attendees. By city social media accounts. The decor becomes the image — and that image reflects on the event organizer.

Locally Twisted has served SLC Pride, the Pride Center, Equality Utah, and the LGBT Chamber of Utah, alongside 8+ city governments across the Wasatch Front. The installations are engineered for outdoor durability: full-day anchoring, weather-tolerant materials, and a clean strike at the end so the venue is left as it was found.

The design centers on the event's color story — whether that's the Progress Flag palette for a Pride celebration, city brand colors for a ribbon cutting, or a neutral civic palette for a public community event.
