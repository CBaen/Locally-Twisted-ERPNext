# Contestant 1 — Round 1 Submission

## Concept Name: "Audience Authority"

The core move in this suite is to make each page feel like it was made
*by someone who has worked that specific buyer's job*. Not designed for
a general audience with a label swap. Designed from the inside of each
buyer's operational reality.

**Civic coordinator:** She's thinking about how the arch photographs
at the parade and whether the venue will hold her responsible for cleanup.

**Marketing director:** He needs brand color matching, AP invoicing,
and proof that LT has worked with brands at his tier.

**Activities director:** She has a gym schedule that doesn't move and
a custodial staff that will not stay late.

**Parent or partner:** She's planning something that matters and she
wants someone who gets that it matters.

The visual differentiation across the suite is deliberate:
- Civic: Slate Blue authority, Pride installation hero, proof grid of 26 orgs
- Corporate: Ink ground, scrolling client crawl, left-rule buyer notes
- School: Slate overlay, named client cards with abbreviations, slate buyer-notes dark band
- Private: Wedding arch hero, portrait gallery, warm palette throughout (warm-white/stone, no slate/ink except final CTA)

Each page has a distinct structural signature even though they share the same
general section-flow beat (hero → proof → story → gallery → audience notes → services/types → CTA).

## Page-by-page summary

**Civic & Community (`/civic-community`)**
Hero on Pride column installation. Slate proof-stats row immediately after hero.
Two-column case study naming SLC Pride, Equality Utah, LGBT Chamber, and 8+
city governments. Stone gallery with wide-aspect civic installation as the
lead image. 26-client named grid (the most proof-dense section in the suite).
Four service cards focused on outdoor durability and civic photography.
Deep Navy CTA.

**Corporate Events (`/corporate-events`)**
Hero on corporate logo arch with ink overlay. Ink crawl bar scrolling all 30
corporate clients (Ancestry to Morgan Stanley to FOX13 to Utah Jazz). Inverted
two-column story (text left, photo right) with Weberstock/broadcast context.
Slate photo gallery. White buyer-notes section with four specific corporate
procurement answers (AP invoicing, brand color, strike, multi-location). Near-white
service cards with navy left-border accent. Ink CTA band with phone number.

**Schools & Campuses (`/schools-campuses`)**
Hero on back-to-school stage installation. Warm-white named-client cards for
UofU, WSU, and St. Joseph's — each with abbreviation and specific event note.
Two-column case story leaning into the specific school operational constraint
("gyms are in use until the hour before the event"). Square-ratio gallery with
all four school-specific images. Service cards with brass bottom-border.
Slate dark buyer-notes band naming school color matching, schedule-tight installs,
clean strike, and PTA budget. Navy CTA.

**Private Celebrations (`/private-celebrations`)**
Hero on wedding organic half-arch (most elevated, most personal image available).
Celebration-type cards covering five types: birthdays, weddings, showers,
milestones, memorials. Portrait-ratio gallery alternating birthday/wedding
installations. Six testimonials from real Google reviews — includes the
memorial review and the seven-year relationship review. Stone buyer-notes
with same-day delivery, at-home/venue, custom, and rush orders. Navy CTA with
phone number for short-notice callers.
