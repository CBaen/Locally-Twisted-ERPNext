# Round 1 Complete — Contestant 1

## Concept: "Audience Authority"

This suite treats each audience page as a capability statement written from
inside that buyer's operational reality. The civic coordinator sees a page that
understands outdoor durability and press photography. The marketing director sees
AP invoicing and brand color matching called out explicitly. The activities director
sees her custodial staff problem solved in the copy. The parent planning a memorial
sees that LT has done this with care before. Every structural and copy decision —
from palette warmth to section ordering to client naming — is audience-specific, not
shared-template with a label swap.

**Civic:** Slate authority, 26-org named grid, Pride installation hero.
**Corporate:** Ink ground, 30-client scrolling crawl, left-rule buyer notes for procurement.
**Schools:** Named client abbreviation cards (UofU/WSU/St. Joseph's), slate operational-constraint buyer notes.
**Private:** Portrait gallery, memorial card included, warmest palette in the suite (warm-white through stone, no ink/slate bands).
