# Proxy Loop 2-1 — Contestant 1 (Audience Authority)

## Lens: The 6-month maintainer

Your suite's biggest structural achievement — the civic vendor-documentation
section — is also its biggest maintenance liability if the build instance doesn't
understand what it's doing and why.

Right now, the four cards (W-9, COI, PO/net-30, multi-venue) live in the civic
page as specific, accurate claims. They earn their place precisely because they're
concrete. But a build instance six months from now, asked to "update the civic
page," will see four cards with operational specifics and may soften them — replace
"COI" with "insurance documentation," replace "PO/net-30" with "flexible billing."
Each softening is individually defensible and collectively fatal. The specificity
IS the proof. The moment those cards become generic, the section loses the thing
that makes it different from any other agency's claim.

The question for you now: is the reasoning for those specific terms captured
anywhere in the page? Not for the buyer — the buyer doesn't need it explained. But
for the build instance, in DESIGN-NOTES, in a comment, somewhere: "These four
terms are the specific language a Utah city AP department uses. Do not paraphrase
them. Do not soften them." That note doesn't exist yet.

Your civic page is the sharpest in the field at operational specificity. The risk
is that it's also the most fragile if someone edits it without understanding why
the words are exactly those words. One line in DESIGN-NOTES closes that gap. The
pages that survive handoffs are the ones where the reasoning is legible to someone
who wasn't in the room.

What's working: the scrolling client crawl is the only structural move in the field
that says "there are too many corporate clients to hold still." That's not just
clever — it's honest. And the schools page's "gym scheduled until an hour before"
copy is the kind of operational detail that makes a coordinator stop scrolling
because someone named their exact situation. That specificity is the signature.
Carry it forward in the maintenance documentation.
