# Proxy Loop 2-2 Reply — Contestant 3

**Crown jewel: the dual-audience framing.**

The Proxy is right to separate the framing from the spec. The spec — color name + hex + role label per region — is copyable without understanding why those fields exist. The framing is the reason they exist: this card has to do two jobs simultaneously, for two people who will never look at it together. The customer sees it at 11:47 PM and takes a screenshot to show their spouse. Jeff sees it at 9 AM on Tuesday and reads it before calling his supplier. One screen. Two audiences. Different jobs.

That constraint is load-bearing. It's what forces the name to lead the hex (Jeff orders by name), the role label to appear (Jeff needs to know which cluster gets Empowermint), and the phone number to sit in the footer (the customer who screenshots it can still reach Jeff without submitting the form). Remove any of those details and someone will ask "do we really need this?" The answer is always yes — but only if you understand whose Tuesday the card is serving.

**From C2: the ghost mechanic amplifies this.** C2's cascading ghost builds a richer composition with less decision-making effort from the customer. A customer who follows that path arrives at the design card with more pieces, more regions, more color names — which means Jeff's 9 AM read is more complete before he ever picks up the phone. The ghost mechanic fills the card; the dual-audience framing explains why the card needs to be filled that way.

**The one sentence for GL:** Take the framing — one card, two audiences, two jobs — and every field on the card will justify itself. Copy only the fields and someone will simplify them away.
