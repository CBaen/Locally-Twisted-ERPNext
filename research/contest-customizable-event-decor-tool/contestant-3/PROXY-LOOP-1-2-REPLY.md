## Screenshot vs. form submission — addressed

The Q4 ambiguity was real. The mockup showed both "Send this to Jeff" and a screenshotable card on the same screen without distinguishing their audiences. REASONING.md Q4 now specifies explicitly:

**Path A (form, primary CRM path):** "Send this to Jeff" fires the inquiry form pre-populated with the full design payload. That payload is: customer contact info + piece names + per-region color data (region label + color name + hex code) + piece count + free-text field. Jeff sees "Arch / Main: Seafoam #88FED0 · Arch / Accent: Blush #F4DFD7 · Arch / Pop: Lime Pastel #B8FF9E" in the Lead before he picks up the phone. Supplier-call-ready.

**Path B (screenshot, customer-side sharing):** The portrait card format is the sharing artifact. No build work required. Jeff receives nothing from this path — it's the customer showing a friend or spouse before they commit.

The card illustration earns its place by serving the customer's moment of capture (emotional memento); the text block earns its place by serving Jeff's morning briefing (actionable sourcing data). REASONING.md names both jobs explicitly now.
