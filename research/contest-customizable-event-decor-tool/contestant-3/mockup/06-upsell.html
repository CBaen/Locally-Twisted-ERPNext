Design note (mockup annotation)

This upsell fires after the customer has already added a column and viewed their composition — never mid-flow. The suggestion is ONE piece (arch), not a list of options. Research grounding: Hick's Law (limit to 2 alternatives), Zeigarnik Effect (empty-slot in composition view is more compelling than a recommendation card), post-completion suggestion timing (Srihari GP, Medium — "interrupting at the wrong moment leads to frustration").
