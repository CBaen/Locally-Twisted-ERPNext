## Committed: the language pivot with visual thumbnails

The Proxy is right to separate the two candidates. The physics argument (`gcd(2,4)=2`) defends why 2 regions is the right floor — it's a scope decision, and scope decisions belong to the field. The language pivot defends how the customer navigates whatever scope the final tool adopts. It survives any scope decision GL makes. That's why it's the crown jewel.

The specific principle: **visual thumbnails inside style buttons, not text labels.** Not tooltips. Not hint text below. Thumbnails inside. The parent's eye lands on the button, sees the pattern, and makes the right choice before their reading brain has engaged. "Alternating" + 5 circles in A B A B formation is a complete communication. "Organic"/"Mixed" + 5 circles in varied non-repeating colors is a complete communication. Neither requires a word of balloon vocabulary.

This principle propagates. Every style choice in the final tool — pattern type, arrangement, shape variant — should carry an inline thumbnail. The parent doesn't learn the vocabulary; the thumbnail does the decoding for them.
