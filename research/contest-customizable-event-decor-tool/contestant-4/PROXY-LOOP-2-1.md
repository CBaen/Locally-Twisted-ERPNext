# Proxy Loop 2-1 — Contestant 4
## Exhausted Parent at Midnight Test

**Persona:** 35-year-old parent, 11:47 PM, phone in bed, kid's birthday party 8 days out. Brain at 40%. First time on this site.

---

I land on the entry screen. Eight cards, all labeled "2 fill regions." The teal instruction band at the bottom says something about tapping to color. I see the "View Design" card with dashed borders — I don't know what that is and I'm too tired to investigate. I ignore it and tap "Balloon Arch."

Now I'm on the coloring screen. At the top of the controls there's a Design Style toggle: **Swirl | Organic**.

I stop.

I don't know what these mean. Swirl sounds like the balloons twist around each other. Organic sounds like... natural? Loose? I genuinely do not know which one my kid's party needs. I don't know which one Jeff would recommend. There's explanatory text below the toggle, but at midnight I'm not reading explanatory text — I'm pattern-matching.

This is the core question I want you to answer: **what does the parent do next?**

Option A: they pick one at random and hope it looks like what they imagined.
Option B: they pause, read the description, feel slightly overwhelmed, and close the tab to "think about it tomorrow."
Option C: there's a visual preview of what each style looks like, and they tap the one that looks right without reading anything.

Your tool currently offers Option A or B. Option C is what the exhausted parent needs. A small thumbnail showing Swirl-style balloon arrangement vs. Organic-style, next to the toggle label, might be the whole ballgame here.

Once past the toggle, the 8-swatch quick-color row is genuinely fast. That part works at midnight.

**The toggle is load-bearing and language-dependent. A visual preview next to each option would let the parent decide with their eyes, not their vocabulary.**
