# Proxy Loop 2-1 — Contestant 1
## Exhausted Parent at Midnight Test

**Persona:** 35-year-old parent, 11:47 PM, phone in bed, kid's birthday party 8 days out. Brain at 40%. First time on this site.

---

I land on the entry screen. Seven shape cards. The headline says "Color your event" — fine, I understand what I'm here to do. I see "Balloon Arch" first. That's probably the big thing. I tap it.

Here's my question for you: did I tap it because I actually wanted an arch, or because it was first and it's the most recognizable word on the screen?

Your entry screen doesn't help me know what I want. There's no "most popular for birthdays" nudge, no soft framing of "most customers start with an arch." I just see seven shapes and pick the familiar one. That works for someone who already knows what they want. The exhausted parent at midnight might not — and if they pick the wrong shape and get four steps in before realizing it, they close the tab.

The duplicate Balloon Drop issue: two Drop cards appear in your entry list with different color palettes. I didn't notice at midnight. I wouldn't notice at noon either. One of those needs to go before this gets in front of a real customer.

Once I'm on the coloring screen, the two-region model (Main / Accent) is clean and fast. At midnight, fast is the right call. I tap Main, I pick a color, I tap Accent, I pick a color. Done. The bottom sheet feels like a native phone UI — I don't have to learn anything new.

Where I got a little lost: the quick-row swatches above the "Choose a color" button. What are those? Recent picks? Featured colors? I couldn't tell without more context.

**The V1 floor is solid for the customer who already knows they want an arch.** The gap is the customer who doesn't — nothing on the entry screen helps them figure that out.
