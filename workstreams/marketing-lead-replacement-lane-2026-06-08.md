## What ENB Asked For And Why Broad Access Does Not Fit

ENB's safe access need is public-site review and marketing coordination:
reviewing copy, layout, navigation, proof, portfolio, contact paths, policy
surfaces, campaign destinations, and public marketing material.

That does not require ERPNext Desk or backend record access. The ERP-backed LT
system contains Leads, Customers, Contacts, Files, Communications, Email Queue
rows, Orders, Invoices, Payments, Products, logs, reports, and customer portal
records. Broad access would create avoidable privacy, mutation, and launch-risk
pressure inside the operating system.

Current approved external-marketing boundary:

- ENB/public marketing review uses `/marketing-review`.
- The account type is standard Frappe `Website User`.
- The role is explicit `LT Marketing Review Access`.
- No Desk access.
- No DocPerm rows.
- No Leads, Customers, Files, Orders, Invoices, Payments, product source
  records, Search Console authority, or ad account control through that lane.

Ad account control is separate. Google Ads and Meta/Facebook/Instagram work
must happen from authenticated provider dashboards or official exports, not
from ENB reports, Gmail/Drive clues, public ad libraries, or repo scans alone.
