# Pricing Formula — Artist Services

Confirmed by Jeff Kimber 2026-04-16 (SpongeBob booking call).

## Formula

- **Balloon twister:** $130 first hour + $115 each additional hour
- **Face painter:** $130 first hour + $115 each additional hour
- **Each artist priced independently** — no bundle discount when combining services
- Example: 1 twister + 1 painter × 2 hrs = (2 × $245) = **$490 subtotal**, tax added at checkout

## Why no combination discount

Each artist is creating custom art for the event. Two artists = twice the creative work. Don't shortchange the experience. **Frame "no combination" proudly — not a fee, a quality commitment.**

## Why this matters

Locally Twisted competes on quality and custom art, not price bundling. Customers often EXPECT a bundle discount — proactive copy must address this before they hit a quote.

## How it lands in the build

- **Pricing calculator** must show per-artist math line-by-line (subtotal per artist, then total)
- **FAQ** must own the "no combination" rule with the quality framing — not as fine print
- **Quote sheets** must break out each artist on separate line items
- **Do NOT offer bundle discounts** in promotions unless Jeff explicitly asks
