# Theme & Character Rules

Confirmed by Jeff Kimber 2026-04-16.

## The rule

- **No theme limits** on face painting or balloon twisting
- Kid asks for it, artists make it: SpongeBob, Patrick, Squidward, unicorn, Spider-Man — anything
- **Minimum experience bar for private parties:** only experienced twisters (years of experience) are sent. They can make anything the kids ask for.

## The selling point

This is a brand differentiator — most competitors limit character catalogs. Locally Twisted's artists have the skill to make whatever the kid dreams up.

## Why this matters

Jeff is proud of his team's skill range. The "any character" capability is why parents choose Locally Twisted over cheaper competitors who limit requests. This needs to be ON the website, not hidden in a phone call.

## How it lands in the build

- **Booking form:** "Theme or character requests?" as a text field — not a dropdown. Let customers type freely.
- **Landing page:** Jeff quote about experienced artists making anything
- **Service pages (face painting + twisting):** highlight "any character, any request"
- **FAQ:** "Can you do [obscure character]? Yes — just ask."
- **Do NOT add copyright warnings or character restrictions in copy** — Jeff explicitly said no limits
