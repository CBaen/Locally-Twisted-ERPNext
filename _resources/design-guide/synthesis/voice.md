# Voice — Synthesis Design

## Voice: Quiet Confidence

Per LT STYLE-GUIDE.md — the three rules applied throughout:

1. **Present tense, not promises.** "Happily delivering along the Wasatch Front" not "We'll deliver to you."
2. **Invite, never push.** "Tell us what you're imagining" not "Call for a free quote."
3. **Warm, not performing.** "Every detail matters" not "We LOVE what we do."

## Headlines by Visitor Type

### Birthday Parents

The birthday parent lands on the hero, scrolls to services, finds the phone number in the nav.
They want to know Jeff can "work the room" and that booking is simple.

| Placement | Headline |
|---|---|
| Hero | "We make celebrations unforgettable." |
| Services page header | "Something for the middle of the party." |
| Balloon Twisting card | "Roaming entertainment at any scale." |
| Face Painting card | "A line they'll wait in happily." |
| FAQ headline | "Practical answers." |
| Booking CTA | "Tell us about yours." |
| Process section | "Booking is straightforward." |

**Voice notes for birthday parent copy:** Warm is allowed here. The subject matter (kids, parties, energy) earns a slightly softer register. Still no exclamation marks. Still no "WE LOVE KIDS." The copy says "works best at gatherings of 50 or more" and "we'll tell you honestly what's available" — confident, specific, no performance.

### Corporate Event Coordinators

Coordinators navigate directly to Work, filter by Corporate, read scale data and process steps.
They want precision and facts, not enthusiasm.

| Placement | Headline |
|---|---|
| Look book header | "The work speaks." |
| Proof strip | "28 years · Wasatch Front · Large scale · Every occasion" |
| Event types row | "Any Event. Any Size." |
| Process step 4 | "We show up and work the room." |
| Configurator step 4 intro | "Scale determines materials, structure, and logistics." |
| Inquiry CTA | "Start a conversation." |

**Voice notes for corporate copy:** No warmth performance. Specificity is the warmth. "One balloon artist handles roughly 30–40 guests per hour depending on design complexity" is more reassuring than any superlative. "A quote follows" is cleaner than "We'll get you a customized pricing package right away."

---

### Wedding Planners

Planners spend the most time with the site — configurator, look book, detail views.
They have mood boards and palettes. They need to feel invited into a creative conversation.

| Placement | Headline |
|---|---|
| Configurator step 1 | "Start with a feeling." |
| Configurator step 2 | "Family colors." |
| Configurator step 3 | "What style?" |
| Configurator step 4 | "What scale?" |
| Post-config summary | "Your configuration" (factual, not superlative) |
| Inquiry redirect | "Continue to inquiry" |
| Look book bottom | "Something else in mind?" |
| Inquiry invitation | "That's exactly the conversation Jeff is built for." |

**Voice notes for wedding planner copy:** The configurator copy does the heavy lifting. "Most balloon decor begins as a mood, not a color list" — this is the line that earns a wedding planner's trust. It says: we know how you think. "No commitment. Jeff follows up with availability and a custom quote" — removes the pressure from the first step.

## Copy that passed the three-rule check

**Proof strip items:**
- "28 years" / "In the work since 1998" — present tense, factual
- "Wasatch Front" / "SL, Davis, Utah & Weber counties" — specific, not promise
- "Large scale" / "Setup in hours, not days" — invite confidence, not urgency

**FAQ answers:**
- "Call if you're last-minute — we'll tell you honestly what's available." — warm, not performing; honest acknowledgment of limits
- "We'll recommend the right team size in your quote." — competence without bravado

**Booking section lead:**
- "A rough picture is enough to start." — removes barrier without removing precision

**Configurator helper text:**
- "Jeff refines the palette with you from there." — craft-first, collaboration-forward
