# Synthesis Complete

The build-target design for Locally Twisted is a four-page Next.js synthesis that transplants D3's editorial density and inquiry-builder pattern, D7's mood-first 4-step configurator and no-text-on-images discipline, and D5's shop page pattern (filter pills, slide-in cart drawer, cross-sell strip) into LT's existing visual system — DM Serif Display headings, Raleway body, Teal `#008080` on CTA buttons only, Near White page ground, Soft Blue footer, and accent palette (Blush through Sky Cyan) appearing exclusively as 48–72px thin bands between sections. The most consequential decisions were: refusing the AI hero default in favor of D3's photo-leads-copy-follows principle; rendering D7's configurator with Near Black active states and blush-tint surface fields instead of amber-copper; keeping D3's `<dl>` spec-table register for the services page while translating it from DM Mono to Raleway; and enforcing loud failure (user-visible error banners with `role="alert"`) on both the configurator submit and the balloon-twisting booking section before any URL serialization to `/inquire`.
