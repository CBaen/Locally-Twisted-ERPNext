Package Language

Use outcome names, not small / medium / large.

  • Opening Day Welcome marks the entrance and ribbon/photo moment.
  • Opening Day Feature supports multiple customer touchpoints.
  • Grand Opening Environment transforms the whole opening space.
  • Custom Opening Celebration covers unusual layouts and premium requests.

Accent pieces may include tabletop balloon accents, floor-standing mini columns, branded color clusters, or photo-area details depending on the venue.

Approval notes: exact color matches depend on balloon availability; outdoor installs require weather and anchoring approval; logos and printed signage are not included unless approved; final layout should follow photos, measurements, or site review.
