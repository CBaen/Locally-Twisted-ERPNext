# Utah Combined Sales Tax Rates — Q2 2026

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Source:** https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/salestax/rate/26q2combined.pdf
**Backup source:** https://tax.utah.gov/business/sales-tax/sales/rates/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-16
**Authority:** Utah State Tax Commission — Utah Code Title 59, Chapter 12, Sales & Use Tax Act
**Scope:** 8-county Wasatch Front service area for Locally Twisted (balloon décor = tangible personal property, full general merchandise rate applies)

## Data Quality Notes

- **All 8 counties confirmed:** Data extracted directly from the official Utah State Tax Commission PDF (26q2combined.pdf, 10 pages, 786 KB, accessed 2026-04-16).
- **No UNKNOWN rates:** Every tax area in the service area has a confirmed rate from the authoritative source.
- **Special districts (MIDA, UIPA, HTRZ, ULA):** Included for completeness. In practice, Locally Twisted balloon deliveries will rarely occur at special district addresses. The underlying city/county rate is the safe default for any ambiguous delivery.
- **ZIP codes:** The PDF does not publish ZIP-to-tax-area mappings in the combined rate table. For ZIP-level mapping, use Utah's ZIP+4 lookup at https://tax.utah.gov/sales/non-nexus or the Streamline Sales Tax database. Note: Park City/Snyderville Basin (ZIP 84098) is a known exception documented in the source.
- **Food rate:** 3.0% statewide — NOT applicable to balloon décor (tangible personal property at full rate).
- **Residential energy rate:** Combined rate reduced by 2.85% — NOT applicable.
